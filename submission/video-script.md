# Video script: Luck or Leak (under 2 minutes)

About 290 spoken words, which is roughly 1 minute 55 seconds at a calm pace.
**Bold** = what you do on screen. Plain text = what you say.

Before recording:
- Open https://nisargsonar.github.io/dashboard-royale/dashboard/ in a private window.
- Use full screen and set zoom to 100%.
- Hide your bookmarks bar.

---

### 0:00 to 0:15 · The problem
**Story, scene 1 (the casino). Click Next to scene 2 (the manager).**

"This is The Gilded Ace, a casino with about 300 slot machines. The manager wants to replace the ten that earned the least, using a hundred-thousand-dollar budget. We asked: is that the right call?"

### 0:15 to 0:28 · Three suspects
**Next to scene 3.**

"A machine can earn little for three reasons: bad luck, a quiet location, or a leak, where it pays out more than it should. Each needs a different fix, so we tested all three."

### 0:28 to 0:50 · The clues
**Next to scene 4 (map), then 5 (hallway), then 6 (M-1158).**

"Clue one: nine of the ten sit in Back Forty, the quietest corner. The same games earn 30 to 45 percent less there.
Clue two: in March its hallway closed. Foot traffic fell 30 percent and never fully came back.
Clue three: one machine, M-1158, pays a win far more often than its design says, every month."

### 0:50 to 1:00 · Two audiences
**Next to scene 7, then "Choose your view". Click "I run the business".**

"We built this for everyone. Managers get plain words and simple examples. Analysts can switch to the math."

### 1:00 to 1:18 · Proof it isn't luck
**Overview: point at the tiles. Click the Machines tab, scroll to the Luck simulator, click "Replay 1,000 times".**

"Here's the proof. We replay M-1158's six months a thousand times on a fair machine. It never does this badly, not once. So it's not luck. It's worth an inspection, not an accusation."

### 1:18 to 1:35 · The model
**Click the Forecast tab. Point at the 4-month chart. Click "Analyst" in the top bar for a second, then back to "Simple".**

"Our forecast uses the weekday and known events. We tested it on four different months it had never seen. It missed by about 47 thousand dollars a day, versus 218 thousand for a simple average. A fancier regression did worse, so we kept it simple."

### 1:35 to 1:55 · The $100k plan
**Click the "$100k plan" tab. Point at the three cards, then click a "+" in the Budget playground.**

"Our advice: don't replace all ten. Spend seventeen and a half thousand dollars. Inspect M-1158, add signs to bring people back to Back Forty, and test the new comp rule on half the guests. Keep the other eighty-two and a half thousand. Bad luck doesn't need fixing. Judges can build their own plan right here, and every claim links to its evidence."

### 1:55 to 2:00 · Close
**Back to the Overview.**

"Luck or Leak. Check before you replace."

**Tips**
- Practise once with a timer. If you run long, shorten the 1:35 section first.
- Speak slowly. Clicks can happen while you talk.
- If the story is already past scene 1, click "▶ Story" in the top bar to restart it.
